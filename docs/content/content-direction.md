# WIMS 360 — Content Direction (author: Shakeel, approved)

This is the VOICE AND STRUCTURE REFERENCE for all site copy. It is illustrative,
not exhaustive — every feature in module-catalog.md must be represented.

## Hard rules
1. SHOW THE SERVICE, NEVER THE SUPPLIER. Never name Terra, Ultrahuman, OpenAI,
   Interakt, Twilio, Brevo, Stripe, Azure AD, or any third-party provider — naming
   them signals dependency. Say "wearable & device connectivity", "WhatsApp
   messaging", "single sign-on", "payments".
2. DEVICE ECOSYSTEMS THE CLIENT OWNS MAY BE NAMED as supported: Apple Health,
   Samsung Health, Fitbit and other supported devices. "and more can be integrated."
3. Name the capability; do not expose implementation detail.
4. Showcase ALL features (see module-catalog.md) — breadth matters.
5. Hedged, careful phrasing: "can", "available", "supported". Never overclaim.
6. HIPAA-aligned / GDPR-ready retained. Never "certified".
7. No prices anywhere. No testimonials until real ones exist.
8. "Client" not "patient". "Practice"/"practitioner" preferred over "clinic"/"clinician".

Understand the whole person. Personalise the journey. Stay connected.
One client. One connected journey. One platform.

WIMS 360 brings investigations, healing, live health data, communication and
intelligent AI together in one connected platform. From the first client history and
clinical assessment to laboratory results, genetics, wearable data, personalised
healing plans and continuous engagement — everything comes together around one
complete client story.

## The Problem
"Your client's health story is bigger than any single report."
Modern longevity and wellness practices collect enormous amounts of information —
clinical assessments, laboratory biomarkers, genetic data, wearables, lifestyle
information, imaging, therapies, appointments, messages, progress over time. When
those pieces live in different systems, the complete story becomes difficult to see.
WIMS 360 connects the journey so your team can spend less time moving information
between systems and more time understanding the client and delivering personalised care.

## Dr.T (Intelligence across the entire journey)
"AI that sees the whole story — not just one report."
Works across the information available within the client's record. Analyses in
seconds, compares findings across history, surfaces relationships and patterns.
Can help practitioners: analyse individual reports; compare results over time;
review broader health information; generate health insights; identify patterns across
multiple sources; assist with case review; draft recommendations for clinician review;
support personalised healing plans; work within a client-specific clinical context.
"Dr.T does not replace the practitioner. It helps the practitioner see more of the
story, faster." Intelligent assistance. Human oversight. Every AI-generated clinical
action remains subject to the appropriate permissions, consent and professional review.

## The Diamond System of Care
"Turn complex information into a clearer picture." Seven dimensions: Diet, Sleep,
Stress, Digestion, Metabolism, Toxicity, Individuality. Softened claim — a structured
way to LOOK ACROSS dimensions; do not claim numeric scoring.
"The objective is not simply to collect more information. It is to help the care team
understand which findings matter, how they relate to each other and what may need
attention next."
